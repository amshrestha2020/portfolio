Prompt Flight Simulator

Pilots rehearse in simulators before they fly. Rehearse your AI prompts the same way: five missions, instant feedback, zero risk. Each prompt is checked against the Context · Task · Constraints method.

  • Context who you are and the situation
  • Task the one thing you want done
  • Constraints limits such as length, time or count
  • Format + guardrails how to answer, and what to do when unsure

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.

      Remember

      • The same prompt can give different answers. Language models are non-deterministic, so precise constraints help, but you still review every output.
      • Give context, a clear task and limits. Vague prompts get generic results.
      • Ask for honesty. Tell the AI to say when it is unsure and to flag claims that need checking against real sources.

      These checks are simple rules, not an AI model. They teach structure, not quality of ideas.
