Flight Simulator

Fly a small plane with real lift, drag, stalls and ground effect. No install, no sign-up. Choose how to start:

Controls
↑↓ nose up / down
←→ bank left / right
WS more / less power
B brake · C camera · P pause · R restart

Also try: 🚁 Helicopter Rotor Lab (how air flows over the blades, with pros and cons) · 🚁 Chopper Airflow Trainer · 🧒 Kids version

Tip: speed is set by the nose angle, climb or descent is set by power. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.
